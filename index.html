How we build

Three promises we can keep.

📡

Works when it matters

Core features run phone to phone, with no connection to a service that can go down. The internet is an optional extra, never a requirement.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics or advertising libraries, and no server of ours that holds your messages. What stays on your phone stays on your phone.

🤝

Honest about limits

If a feature shares anything with a third party, we say so in plain words inside the app and in the policy. No "military-grade" hand-waving.
