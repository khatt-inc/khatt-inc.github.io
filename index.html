how we build

Three promises we keep in code.

Architecture decisions, not slogans. Each one is something you can check.

01

Works where it matters

Core features run device to device and on device. The internet is an enhancement, never a single point of failure.

02

Private by design

No tracker SDKs, no ad networks, no quiet data warehouse. What belongs on your phone stays on your phone.

03

Honest about limits

We publish what our products do and what they cannot do, in plain words. No inflated claims.
