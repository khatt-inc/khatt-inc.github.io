1. Summary

Khatt ED-AI is in a pilot stage with a small group of teachers, tutors and learning centres. This policy describes what the pilot does today and will be updated before any wider launch.
  • Minimal data: We collect only what the workspace needs to run lessons, quizzes, study-room questions and fee records.
  • No advertising: No ad networks, no tracking across other websites, and we do not sell personal data.
  • Grounded answers: Study-room answers are drawn from teacher-approved lesson material and cite the source page.
  • Teacher review: Generated quiz questions are drafts until a teacher approves them.
  • Not used to train our models: Khatt does not train AI models on student or teacher content.

2. Information we collect

We process information strictly necessary for providing the educational workspace:

  • Teacher accounts: Name, email address, workspace details, and the lesson files (such as PDFs) you upload.
  • Student accounts: Name or student identifier, course enrolment, quiz attempts and results, and the questions asked in the study room.
  • Parent accounts: The link to the student you are authorised to follow, the progress shown to you, and the payment records for that student.
  • Operational logs: Basic logs, error reports and usage and cost counters needed to run and secure the service.

3. AI processing

Khatt ED-AI uses language models to draft quiz questions from uploaded lesson files and to answer student questions from teacher-approved material.

  • Grounding: Answers are built from passages retrieved from the teacher's lesson material and cite the page and excerpt they came from. Where the material does not support an answer, the system should say so rather than guess.
  • Model providers: We run open-weight models on our own server where possible. Some AI features may fall back to a hosted third-party model provider, in which case the relevant lesson text or question is sent to that provider under its terms. We will list providers here before the pilot widens.
  • No training by Khatt: We do not use your content to train or fine-tune models.
  • Teacher approval: Generated questions are drafts. Nothing is shown to students until the teacher approves it. Model output can be wrong, so review it.

4. Payment records

The billing view lets a workspace record fees and the payments made against them.

  • A record holds the amount (EGP), date, status and the method used: Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, Fawry or cash.
  • The pilot does not ask you to type card numbers or banking PINs into Khatt ED-AI. Payment providers we may connect later will be listed here first.

5. Access & separation

Each teacher or centre works in a separate workspace.

  • Access to courses, lesson files, student rosters and results is enforced on the server by role (teacher, student, parent) and by workspace.
  • Lesson files and student records are not visible to other teachers' workspaces.

6. Parent communication

Parent progress messaging, for example over WhatsApp, is not enabled in the pilot. We will enable it only after parent identity linking, consent and messaging rules are defined, and we will update this policy first.

7. Retention & deletion

Educators and institutions own their content.

  • You can ask us to delete your account, lesson files or student records by emailing privacy@khatt.app.
  • Formal retention periods and self-service export and deletion tools are being defined for the pilot and will be published here.

8. Students & children

Khatt ED-AI is intended for use with a teacher or learning centre.

  • Student accounts are created by, or with the approval of, the teacher or centre running the course.
  • We do not profile students for advertising or marketing.
  • Parents can ask to review or delete their child's records through their teacher or centre, or by contacting privacy@khatt.app.

9. Contact & inquiries

For questions or requests regarding student privacy, data protection, or enterprise pilot contracts:

Email: privacy@khatt.app
Organization: Khatt Inc.
