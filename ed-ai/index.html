For teachers

From lesson file to reviewed quiz.

Upload your PDF lesson materials and Khatt ED-AI drafts multiple-choice questions from the source. You stay in charge: review, edit, approve or reject each question, then publish it to your students.

The Teacher Command Center also shows quiz outcomes and student activity, so you can see who needs attention.

  • Draft MCQs from your files
  • Review, edit, approve
  • Student activity and results

For students

A study companion that shows its sources.

Students open an enrolled course, read the lesson and ask questions in the Study Room. Answers are grounded in the teacher-approved material, with the page and the exact excerpt cited so a student can check it for themselves.

When they are ready, they take the teacher's published quiz and see their results.

Ask, read, check.

The interactive study room keeps the conversation tied to the lesson, so questions about the chapter get answers from the chapter.

For parents & fees

Progress you can see, fees you can track.

Parents linked to their child see the progress they are authorised to see. The billing view records each payment by Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, Fawry or cash, so dues and receipts are no longer scattered across chats and notebooks.

WhatsApp progress reports are on our roadmap and will launch only after parent consent and messaging rules are in place.

The principles we are building the pilot around.

01

Teacher in the loop

Generated questions are drafts. Nothing reaches students until the teacher has reviewed and approved it.

02

Grounded and cited

Study-room answers come from teacher-approved material and point to the page and excerpt they came from.

03

Separate workspaces

Each teacher or centre works in their own workspace, with server-side access rules between courses and teachers.

Early access

Join the ED-AI pilot.

We are onboarding a small group of teachers, tutors and learning centres in Egypt. Pilot participants get early access to the Teacher Command Center and hands-on onboarding. Features may change as we learn from the pilot.
