1. Summary

Rasid is a private, offline personal finance tracker designed for total user sovereignty.
  • Zero account required: No email, no phone registration, no passwords registered on any server.
  • Zero cloud servers: Khatt operates no backend servers, databases, or API gateways for Rasid.
  • Zero analytics or advertising SDKs: No Google Analytics, Firebase Telemetry, Meta Pixels, or crash reporting beacons.
  • 100% on-device data: All accounts, transactions, and categories remain solely on your physical device.
  • No network permission: Rasid does not declare or request the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission.

2. Data stored on your device

Rasid stores only the financial information you create or choose to ingest:

  • Account records: Account names, currency codes, institutions (e.g., CIB, NBE, Vodafone Cash, Cash in Wallet), and initial balances.
  • Ledger transactions: Date, amount in minor units (piastres), source and destination accounts, category tags, and optional user notes.
  • Preferences and local progress: Language, digit style, theme, tracked banks, and optional streak and badge progress. These stay on the device and the streak features can be switched off.
  • Encryption key: A random key generated on your device and kept in your device's protected storage. It is never transmitted.

3. SMS permissions & on-device parsing

To reduce manual data entry, Rasid offers an automated SMS parser for bank and wallet notifications in Egypt.

  • Scope of permission: If enabled, Rasid requests read-only access to SMS messages (android.permission.READ_SMS and android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS).
  • Sender allowlist: The parser only handles messages from the financial senders you track (for example CIB, NBE, Banque Misr, QNB, Vodafone Cash and InstaPay). Messages from other senders are not processed.
  • On-device processing: Parsing happens on your phone. Messages received while the app is closed wait in a local queue until you review them, and the message text is deleted once you review it. Nothing is transmitted externally.
  • User approval gate: Parsed messages are routed to a local Review Queue. A transaction is only committed to your ledger after you verify, edit, or approve it.

4. Zero network communication

Rasid is architected with a physical barrier to data exfiltration: it completely omits the INTERNET permission in its Android manifest.

  • The app cannot initiate HTTP/HTTPS requests, socket connections, or DNS queries.
  • Even in the event of an internal fault or vulnerability, user data cannot be uploaded to the cloud.

5. Database encryption

Rasid encrypts your ledger on your device.

  • Key handling: The encryption key is generated once on your device and stored in its protected storage. Khatt never receives it.
  • Integrity: Stored data is protected against tampering as well as against being read.

6. Encrypted portable backups

Rasid allows users to export and import complete ledger archives:

  • Backups are saved as .rasid files protected by encryption, using a passphrase you choose at export time.
  • You control where backup files are stored (e.g., local storage, USB drive, or personal cloud). Khatt has no copy of your backup files or encryption passphrases.

7. Biometrics & app lock

Rasid supports Android's standard BiometricPrompt API (fingerprint / face unlock) and PIN locking. Biometric verification is processed by your device's secure hardware element; biometric templates are never exposed to Rasid or Khatt.

8. Data retention & deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you have immediate and permanent control over retention:

  • You can clear individual accounts or transactions at any time within the app.
  • "Erase all data" in Settings permanently deletes all accounts, transactions and settings after a typed confirmation.
  • Uninstalling Rasid from your Android device immediately deletes all local databases and cached keys.

9. Children's privacy

Rasid does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a local utility tool designed for personal and family budgeting.
