How it works

Your financial privacy, guarded on your phone.

Rasid turns bank notifications into clean accounting without any third-party aggregation service.

  1. Read on the device

    Rasid reads transaction messages from the Egyptian banks and wallets you choose to track. Parsing happens on your phone; message text is never uploaded.

  2. Review before it counts

    Every message lands in a review queue with merchant, amount and account filled in, plus a confidence score. Nothing touches your ledger until you approve it, and low-confidence messages always wait for you.

  3. Post to a balanced ledger

    Approved items post as integer piastres into an encrypted local database. Balances are always derived from your records, never typed in directly.

Core features

Built for real accounting, not guesswork.

01

Egyptian banks & wallets, natively

A parser tuned for Egyptian notification formats, with confidence scoring, balance hints and de-duplication. It handles Arabic-Indic digits and covers CIB, NBE, Banque Misr, QNB, Vodafone Cash and InstaPay messages.

02

Encrypted at rest

Your ledger is encrypted on your phone, and the key never leaves it.

03

No rounding errors

Money is stored as integer piastres (قروش). 100.50 EGP is 10050. Floating-point types are never used for amounts.

04

No internet permission

Rasid does not request internet access, and it has no analytics or crash-report SDKs. There is no server for your data to reach.

05

Portable encrypted backups

Export a .rasid archive protected with your own passphrase and restore it on another phone. CSV export is available too.

06

Reconcile and understand

Enter the real balance and Rasid records a single adjustment for the difference. Monthly reports show spending and income donuts, six-month trends, savings rate and a few plain-language insights.

07

App lock

Protect Rasid with a PIN and optional biometrics, and hide balances on the home and accounts screens.

Accounting integrity

The seven ledger rules.

Rasid's ledger engine validates these rules on every write, in addition to the checks in the interface. They keep balances honest no matter how a transaction arrives.

Rule 1Derived balances. Balance = starting balance + completed credits − completed debits + adjustments. Never edited directly.
Rule 2Transfer invariance. A transfer is one record; it moves the exact amount between accounts and never changes the total.
Rule 3Balance isolation. Pending, failed, excluded and deleted items never touch balances.
Rule 4Report exclusions. Adjustments change account balances but stay out of monthly income and expense totals.
Rule 5Refund linking. A refund links to its original expense and reduces net spending instead of inflating income.
Rule 6Soft deletes. Deleting hides a record but keeps history, so every delete can be undone.
Rule 7Colour invariance. Amber marks the brand and things waiting for you, never money in or out.
Made for Egypt

Arabic first, English ready.

Switch between Arabic (right-to-left) and English, choose Western or Eastern Arabic digits, and pick a light, dark or system theme. Every transaction has a detail sheet to edit, re-categorise, record a refund, add a fee, or delete with undo.
