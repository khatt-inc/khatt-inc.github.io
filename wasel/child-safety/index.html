1. Our position

Khatt has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion and trafficking of minors. Wasel must not be used for any of these.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to sexual or sexualized content involving a minor.
  • Grooming, or soliciting sexual content or contact from a minor.
  • Sextortion, or threats to share intimate images of anyone, including minors.
  • Advertising or facilitating child trafficking or exploitation.

Wasel's in-app community rules, which every user accepts before using public channels, also prohibit anything illegal, sexual, violent or harassing.

3. How Wasel works, and what that means for safety

  • Wasel has no accounts and no Wasel-operated message server. Private chats are end-to-end encrypted between the people in them, so Khatt cannot read them.
  • Public content (the public feed and area channels) is visible to people nearby and, in area channels, to anyone who joins the area. Area channels use third-party relays that Khatt does not operate.
  • Because of this design, Khatt cannot proactively scan or remove user content. Our protections are the in-app tools below, the age requirement, and our response to reports.

4. In-app tools

  • Report: on public posts and area-channel posts, the Report action hides the post on your phone.
  • Mute: hide any person's posts on your phone.
  • Community rules: shown and accepted before public channels are used.

5. Reporting to Khatt

If you see or experience CSAE in or connected to Wasel, email khatt.inc@gmail.com with what you saw, where (feed, area channel, chat) and when. Do not forward the material itself. Reports are reviewed by a person.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. You can also report CSAM to your national hotline or to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (report.cybertip.org).

6. Action and law enforcement

  • We act on credible reports as far as our design allows, including by working with relay operators and app stores.
  • Where we become aware of CSAM, we report it to the relevant authorities, including NCMEC in the United States and national authorities elsewhere as the law requires, and we cooperate with valid law-enforcement requests.
  • We keep this page and our practices under review as laws and Google Play policy change.

7. Age requirement

Wasel is for people aged 16 and over and is not directed to children.

8. Contact
