How it works

Phones become the network.

No towers, no servers, no accounts.

Find people near you

Wasel finds other Wasel phones over Bluetooth Low Energy. Add a friend in person by scanning their QR code, which also protects you from impostors.

Send a note

Write a message or hold to record a voice note. It's sealed with end-to-end encryption on your phone before it leaves.

Phones pass it on

Nearby phones carry sealed envelopes onward without being able to read them, until they reach the right person.

Features

Small app, big toolbox.

💬

Messages & voice notes

Private chats with friends, plus a nearby feed for everyone around you. Voice notes are encrypted and go only to the person you send them to.

🛡️

Noise protocol encryption

Direct messages use the Noise Protocol Framework with Curve25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305 and BLAKE3. Relaying phones can't read, change, or identify what they carry.

✅

"I'm safe" check-in

Let trusted contacts and neighbours nearby know you're okay with one tap.

🕶️

Stealth mode

Receive messages without showing your device to others. Rotating identifiers make tracking harder.

🧽

Fresh start

One hold-to-confirm action wipes keys, chats and contacts from your phone.

🌍

Area channels (optional)

Turn on the internet relay to chat with everyone in a map cell. It's off by default, and it's public: area posts, your nickname and the cell you join are visible to everyone in that area, and relay operators can see your IP address.

🕊️

Carrier Pigeon game

A built-in pigeon adventure with 16 worlds and local multiplayer races, because waiting for signal shouldn't be boring. Available in 20 languages, including Arabic with full right-to-left layout.
