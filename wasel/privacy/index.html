1. Summary

Wasel is a peer-to-peer messenger that works over Bluetooth, and optionally over the local network, Wi-Fi Aware and the internet.
  • No account. You give no email, phone number, name or password.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No Wasel-operated server. Khatt does not receive your messages, contacts, keys or location.
  • Messages, keys and contacts are stored only on your device.
  • Two optional features send data to third parties: the Internet relay and Area channels (sections 4 and 5). Both are off until you use them.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Identity keys: Curve25519 key pairs are generated locally on first launch and kept in the app's protected storage.
  • Messages, voice notes, contacts, settings: stored locally in the app sandbox.
  • Fresh start: Settings > Fresh start permanently erases keys, conversations, contacts and settings from your device. It cannot recall anything already published to third-party relays (section 5).

3. Bluetooth mesh and end-to-end encryption

  • Wasel talks directly device-to-device over Bluetooth Low Energy.
  • Private messages use the Noise Protocol Framework (Noise_XX_25519_ChaChaPoly_BLAKE3). Only the intended recipient can read them.
  • Intermediate devices relay sealed envelopes and cannot read their content.
  • Nearby devices can estimate how close you are from Bluetooth signal strength while discovery is on. Stealth mode stops your device from being shown.
  • Nearby feed posts are visible to nearby people and the devices that relay them. Do not post anything you want to keep private.

4. Optional internet relay (Nostr)

Off by default. When you turn on Settings > Internet relay:

  • The app opens TLS WebSocket (wss://) connections to third-party public relays: relay.damus.io, nos.lol and relay.primal.net.
  • Relay operators can see your IP address and when you connect. Khatt does not operate these relays or control their logging.
  • Private mesh messages travel through relays as end-to-end encrypted envelopes that relays cannot read.

The app can also exchange sealed envelopes with other Wasel devices on the same local network (UDP, local network only) and over Wi-Fi Aware; neither goes through the internet.

5. Area channels (public)

Area channels need the internet relay and are public.

  • When you join, the app publishes to the relays the geohash cell (1–6 characters; a cell chosen with "Use my location" is 5 characters, about 5 km), your nickname and a cell-scoped public key. Anyone in the same cell can see them.
  • Everything you post in the channel is public to everyone who joins the cell.
  • Posts on third-party relays cannot be recalled or deleted by you or by Khatt.
  • You must accept the community rules (no illegal, hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or private content about others) before your first post or channel join. You can mute, hide or report any author. These actions hide content on your phone only, because Wasel has no server to moderate.

6. Location

  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION is requested only when you tap "Use my location" in an Area channel.
  • The app takes one coarse fix, converts it to a 5-character geohash cell (about 5 km) and discards the coordinates. Coordinates are never stored or sent. Only the cell is shared with the channel (section 5).
  • Bluetooth permissions are declared neverForLocation. On Android 11 and older the system requires a location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, limited to Android 11 and older) for Bluetooth scanning; Wasel does not read your location for it.

8. In-app tips (Google Play Billing)

Wasel has an optional tip jar. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. Wasel does not receive or store card or bank details. Tips unlock nothing.

9. Age requirement

Wasel is for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed to children. Public area channels can show user-generated content.

10. Safety notice

Wasel is not a substitute for emergency services. Do not rely on it to reach emergency help.
